DAILY NARRATIVE ENGINE
================================================================================
Every day classify what KIND of day is forming:

Six day-type classifications:

1. TREND DAY
   Conditions: Strong HTF bias, clear objective, Asia builds stable range
               London/NY expands in trend direction
   Behavior: Favor continuation entries, allow pullback models
   
2. EXPANSION DAY
   Conditions: Tight compression, untapped liquidity, delivery beginning
   Behavior: Favor breakout/repricing models, expect strong displacement
   
3. REVERSAL DAY
   Conditions: Liquidity sweep completed, HTF extreme reached
               Structure shift appears right after sweep
   Behavior: Favor reversal models, require stronger confirmation
   
4. RANGE DAY
   Conditions: No clean HTF objective, price trapped in well-defined range
               Weak displacement, repeated liquidity tapping
   Behavior: Reduce trend-following, allow mean-reversion only
             Lower trade frequency, tighten quality bar
   
5. NEUTRAL DAY
   Conditions: Mixed signals across timeframes, no dominant narrative
               Weak session intent, conflicting readings
   Behavior: BLOCK most trades, only allow exceptional setups
   
6. NEWS DAY
   Conditions: High-impact scheduled event, compression pre-release
               Abnormal behavior post-release
   Behavior: Delay/block trading around event, require post-news stabilization

- TECHNICAL

- Watches for tight compression/consolidation
- Enters on breakout into expansion
- EARLIEST entry timing
- HIGHEST false-start risk

- AGGRESSIVE

SMART MONEY CONCEPTS ENGINE
================================================================================
Implement complete SMC framework:

Break of Structure (BOS)
- Price breaks previous significant high/low in trend direction
- Confirms trend continuing

Change of Character (CHoCH)
- First break against prevailing trend
- Early warning sign reversal might start
- Not yet confirmed

Market Structure Shift (MSS)
- Stronger, more decisive than CHoCH
- Real displacement behind it
- Closer to confirmation

Internal vs External Structure
- Internal: Smaller, frequent swings inside range (noise-prone)
- External: Larger, range-defining swings (what actually matters)
- Distinction prevents treating minor wiggles same as major breaks

Protected High / Protected Low
- High/low unlikely to be taken out soon
- Breaking it would contradict confirmed structure

Premium Swing / Discount Swing
- Swings specifically in premium or discount zone

SCORING STRUCTURE BREAKS:
- Displacement (how strong/fast away from swing)
- Follow-through (did price keep going or stall?)
- Reaction quality (sharp wick vs messy grind)
- Liquidity taken (actual stops triggered?)
- Body-confirmation (not just wick poking through)
- Protection formed (new protected high/low?)

FAIR VALUE GAP ENGINE
================================================================================
Imbalance zones created by fast moves:

Fair Value Gap Definition
- Bullish FVG: High of candle 1 < Low of candle 3 (with candle 2 as displacement)
- Bearish FVG: Low of candle 1 > High of candle 3 (mirror)
- Empty space represents unfinished business

FVG Types:
- Bullish FVG / Bearish FVG (base)
- Nested FVG (smaller FVG inside larger one)
- Inversion FVG (failed FVG that flips role)
- Consecutive FVG (multiple stacked, stronger signature)
- Balanced Price Range (BPR) (overlapping bull + bear FVG)

Fill States:
- Unfilled (completely untouched)
- Partially filled (partway into gap)
- Filled (fully traded through)
- Invalid (broken decisively)

Quality Scoring:
- Size (larger gap = stronger imbalance)
- Age (fresher = higher weight)
- Momentum (strength of displacement candle)
- Trend alignment
- Session location

TRADING EFFECT:
- Entry refinement zone (precise price within broader context)
- Target (where move statistically likely to react)


LIQUIDITY SWEEP ENGINE
================================================================================
Real-time detection of liquidity raids:

Sweep Types:
- Buy-side raid / Sell-side raid (directional label)
- Stop hunt (generic behavioral category)
- Liquidity grab (surgical, single-wick version)
- Engineered liquidity (deliberately built up beforehand)
- Single/Double/Triple sweep (how many attempts/pools)
- Failed sweep (didn't actually take level out)
- Liquidity vacuum (sparse thin area)

MECHANICAL DETECTION:
Price trades beyond tracked level (wick or penetration)
THEN closes back inside prior range (key: snap-back defines sweep)
Closes that hold beyond = closer to genuine breakout
Closes that snap back = signature of sweep

Classification AFTER Detection:
- Reversal (sweep + structure shift opposite direction) ← STRONGEST
- Continuation (sweep + original direction resumes)
- Distribution/Accumulation (sweep + sideways absorption)
- Fake breakout (sweep + reversal back through level)

CRITICAL RULE:
Every sweep stored with outcome classification
Not just "successful" sweeps
Used later for Adaptive Engine pattern discovery

POSITION MANAGEMENT ENGINE
================================================================================
Live trade management from entry to close:

Management Functions:

Partial Take Profit:
- Close portion at TP1 (nearest valid target)
- Let remainder ride to TP2 (larger objective)
- Timing: when TP1 hits or confirmed within range

Break-Even Movement:
- Move stop to entry once sufficient profit accrued
- Gate to: after TP1 hits OR after X risk-multiples earned
- Prevent early BE movement (kills trades on retracement)

Trailing Stop (Structure-Based):
- Move stop to just beyond most recent protected swing
- Or ATR-adjusted trail (tighter low vol, wider high vol)
- Don't trail through noise, only confirmed swings

Target Reduction:
- If objective nearly complete, tighten TP
- Avoid low-probability chasing past delivered objective
- Session-aware (don't carry past session boundary)

Exit on Invalidation:
- If protected level breaks before stop, exit immediately
- Setup reasoning invalidated = exit trigger (before SL hit)
- Don't wait for arbitrary SL price

Time Exit:
- Day-trader doesn't expect multi-session holds
- Exit if no development within expected window
- Prevent "zombie" trades going nowhere for hours

Session-End Exit:
- Flatten before session boundary (if configured)
- Avoid carrying into different liquidity/volatility profile

Emergency Exit:
- Triggered by extreme news or abnormal spread
- Live mirror of Engine 1's entry-time environment gate
- Pre-entry condition deterioration = mid-trade exit

HANDOFF: On full close, complete lifecycle record goes to Statistics Engine
